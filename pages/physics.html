How to build a time machine

Equipment

  1. 1 black hole
  2. 1 wormhole
  3. 1½ galaxies' worth of dark energy (ish)

Instructions

  1. Open A.
  2. A time machine requires you to travel through space and time. Stretch B between your location and the destination in space, using C to keep B open.
  3. Move further or closer to A's event horizon to adjust how far you go in time.
  4. When you are finished adjusting, jump through B and you have arrived!
